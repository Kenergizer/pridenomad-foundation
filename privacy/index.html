Privacy Policy

Plain language. No tricks.

Last updated: June 2026

The short version: we collect your email address when you subscribe to our newsletter. We use it to send you the newsletter. We do not sell it. We do not share it with advertisers. We are a nonprofit — your inbox is not our product.

What we collect

When you subscribe to the PrideNomad Foundation newsletter, we collect your email address. That is the only personal information we require. You may optionally provide your name, but it is not required to subscribe.

Our website uses standard analytics to understand how visitors find and use our content. This includes page views, referral sources, and general geographic region (country level). We do not use advertising tracking pixels.

How we use your information

  • To send you our weekly newsletter (you asked for it; that's why we have it)
  • To understand how our content is being used so we can improve it
  • To communicate important updates about the Foundation

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles. We do not sell your information to third parties. We do not share your information with sponsors or commercial partners.

Newsletter and email

Our newsletter is delivered through Beehiiv, a newsletter platform. When you subscribe, your email address is stored in Beehiiv's system. Beehiiv's privacy policy governs their handling of your data. You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any newsletter issue — no explanation required, no friction.

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies for basic analytics. These are anonymous and aggregate — we cannot identify individual visitors from this data. We use this information to understand which guides and research pages are most useful to our readers. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; this will not affect your ability to access any of our content.

Data retention

We retain subscriber email addresses as long as the newsletter is active and the subscriber remains subscribed. When you unsubscribe, your data is removed from our active list. You may request permanent deletion of your data by contacting us at the address below.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to request its deletion, and to withdraw consent for any use of it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@pridenomadFoundation.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at privacy@pridenomadFoundation.com. We're a small nonprofit and we read every message.
